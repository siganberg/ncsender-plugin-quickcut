Keyhole

Hanging slot with a keyhole bit: plunge, cut the slot, retrace to the hole, retract.

Slot

The ring in the Origin picker is the plunge hole (the first one, with a pattern); the dots are the corners, edges, and center of the whole cut. The slot runs Slot Length from the hole. Set Bit Diameter to the keyhole bit’s head diameter.

Depth

One plunge to full depth at the Plunge Rate. The slot is always a single pass — a shallower pass would cut the narrow neck slot out to the full head width. Depth is to the bottom of the head.
